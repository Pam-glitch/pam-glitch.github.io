about

Pronouns: they/them  ·  Based in Oslo, Norway  ·  Italian / Norwegian

I am a composer, artist, vocalist, and performer specialising in interdisciplinary works that blend music, visual art, and theatre. My compositional practice explores the intersection of sound, movement, voice, and technology.

I trained in composition primarily with Anders Tveit, Kristine Tjøgersen and Sivan Eldar, and in classical Bel Canto singing in Norway and Italy — focusing on opera and theatre — with Elizabeth N. Schulz. With a background in visual composition from Liceo Artistico Amedeo Modigliani di Giussano (IT), I integrate visual symbolism into my artistic work.
